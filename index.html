Physics-Informed Machine Learning

Chemisorption is widely used to capture CO₂ from industrial gas streams, but predicting its behaviour over time remains challenging. In this work, Physics-informed Neural Ordinary Differential Equation (Neural ODE) models were developed to predict CO₂ absorption and chemical consumption in a packed-bed system. Additionally, physics guided-enhancements were incorporated into the models through soft constraints in the training loss function and additional collocation points, enabling the models to learn from both experimental data and governing physical behaviour. Rigorous data ablation and statistical significance tests were conducted as well.

Computational Fluid Dynamics

Fluidized beds are widely used across industries, yet their behaviour with non-Newtonian fluids remains less understood than that of conventional Newtonian systems. Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), specifically ANSYS Fluent was employed, to model the complex interactions between solid particles and non-Newtonian fluids. The study investigates fluidization behaviour, including particle velocity, voidage and heat transfer, with a particular focus on understanding how flow pulsation influences heat transfer performance.

In collaboration withNational University of Singapore

Computer-Aided Design Formulation

Formulating to a specification is normally a matter of trial batches: dose the ingredients, measure the result, adjust, repeat. This work closes that loop around a learned model. Taking colour matching as the worked case, three primary pigments are dosed in some ratio, the mixed result is measured against a target, and an optimiser trained on the measurements taken so far proposes the next recipe rather than a human guessing it. The model never sees the underlying mixing law — it learns the recipe-to-property map from the batches themselves, and converges on a formulation within tolerance in a handful of iterations instead of a full factorial screen.

In collaboration withNational University of Singapore

Catenane Ring Dynamics

This study investigates the dynamics of two interlocked catenane rings under Brownian motion and elongational flow. Their relaxation behaviour and response to flow are characterised using relaxation time and the Weissenberg number, with comparisons made against linear polymer chains. The work explores how polymer topology and interlocking influence molecular motion and deformation under different flow conditions.

Hyperspectral Imaging & Deep Learning

Characterising the composition of polymer waste is essential for improving plastic recycling and enabling more effective material recovery. This work combines hyperspectral imaging (HSI) with deep learning to simultaneously identify and quantify multiple additives in polypropylene-based polymer blends. The approach enables the detection of components such as polyethylene, talc and flame retardants from their spectral signatures, while predicting their concentrations and presence within a single framework.

Food-Waste Valorisation

This work explores the conversion of food waste into a valuable feedstock for lactic acid production. Moisture and pathogens are first removed using a treatment technology developed by Cora Environment, followed by the extraction of glucose from the processed material. The glucose-rich substrate is then fermented using Lactobacillus helveticus to produce lactic acid, demonstrating a potential pathway for transforming food waste into higher-value bioproducts.

In collaboration withCova Environment
